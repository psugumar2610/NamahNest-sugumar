About me

I’m Sugumar Parthasarathy, I was born in Chennai and working as Senior Development Lead with 6+ years in IT. and have always been deeply connected to spirituality and personal growth. The serenity and devotion of Thiruvannamalai Temple have been a guiding light in my life, teaching me patience, humility, and the power of quiet perseverance. Visiting the temple and experiencing its sacred atmosphere inspires reflection, mindfulness, and a sense of inner peace that I carry into my daily life.

My life philosophy resonates with these words: "Work hard in silence, believe in the process, and let your success shout your story."

Outside of work and spirituality, I enjoy exploring new experiences, learning from the world around me, and cherishing the relationships that enrich my life.
